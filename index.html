    Arrows drift downwind, faster in stronger wind. They run at 6× the real speed so the motion is easy to see.

    Three dinghies beat and run and four windsurfers reach across the wind, at least 250 m off the east shore. They're drawn 6× life size and sail at realistic speeds for the wind, sped up 6× like the arrows. Each is a little faster or slower than the others, and they slow to 0.6× in the Novice Area.

    Moving a slider switches to wind you set yourself, which is handy for teaching points of sail. Live readings come from one station, so the arrows show the same wind across the whole basin. Real wind varies near the breakwaters and the shore.

      About this map

      For orientation and teaching only. Depths, shoals and silting change. Check the tide and the official NOAA chart before you sail.

      If you share your location, it's used only in your browser to place you on the map. It isn't sent or saved anywhere.

      • Buildings, docks, roads: © OpenStreetMap contributors via OpenFreeMap
      • Aerial photos: USDA NAIP via USGS The National Map (public domain)
      • Live wind: CSC weather station

      • Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles
      • Chart: NOAA Office of Coast Survey, ENC Online
      • Bridges: tower and pier positions, outlines and clearances from NOAA ENC data; heights and spans from goldengate.org and published figures; ground at the bridge ends from USGS 3DEP via OpenTopoData. Simplified models at true height.
